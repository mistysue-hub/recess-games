Build Logs

Six games. Twelve builds. One junior dev: you.

Every build is a page from the studio's log. Read it, build along in VS Code, ship the required features on your own, then show the lead dev it runs.

  1. Build 01Day One at the Studioa window, a square named Blip that you can steer, and your first Lua.v0.1.0 · Blip
  2. Build 02Meteor Drift, part 1a ship, a sky full of falling rocks, fuel to grab, a score, and the one trick behind every game: lists of things. (Next build, the game learns to insult you.)v0.2.0 · Meteor Drift
  3. Build 03Meteor Drift, part 2a title screen, a game-over screen, real art, your own sound effects, a bigger font, and a difficulty ramp. Meteor Drift becomes a finished game.v0.3.0 · Meteor Drift
  4. Build 04Tower Hop, part 1gravity, a jump, a tower of platforms that builds itself forever, a camera that follows you up, and stars that drift at two speeds.v0.4.0 · Tower Hop
  5. Build 05Tower Hop, part 2a hero made of real pixels that actually runs, a burst of particles on every jump, screen shake when you fall, and a high score that survives closing the game.v0.5.0 · Tower Hop
  6. Build 06Circuit Breaker, part 1a grid of pipe tiles, a mouse that rotates them, a tween you write yourself, and a flood-fill that knows when the power gets through.v0.6.0 · Circuit Breaker
  7. Build 07Circuit Breaker, part 2levels that live in text files, a next-level screen, undo, a flash when the power lands, and three levels nobody but you has ever played. Circuit Breaker becomes a finished game.v0.7.0 · Circuit Breaker
  8. Build 08Lantern Keep, part 1a keep drawn from a text file, a hero who can't walk through walls, a camera that follows them, and your first class.v0.8.0 · Lantern Keep
  9. Build 09Lantern Keep, part 2wisps with moods, a dialog box that types itself out, a bag, an iron key, and a door that cares whether you have it. Lantern Keep becomes a finished game.v0.9.0 · Lantern Keep
  10. Build 10Stack Attack, part 1a physics world, a crane that never stops moving, crates that tumble and settle like real crates, and a score that climbs as high as your tower does.v0.10.0 · Stack Attack
  11. Build 11Stack Attack, part 2a hook that swings, a menu with a settings screen you build from scratch, music that loops, and a game you can hand to another human as one file.v0.11.0 · Stack Attack
  12. Build 12Your Gamenothing from me. Today the studio ships something of yours.v1.0.0 · Your Game
