# Case 004: Two Princes, One Tower (optional)

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: Optional: in 1483 two young princes in the Tower of London were never seen again. The learner weighs 500 years of sources by who wrote them, when and why.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- explain why a source written at the time is usually stronger than one written decades later
- ask who wrote a source and who they were writing for

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Optional: skip it if tutor notes or the guardian say so (mark M08 skipped, say the next case is ready). Unsolved: never say what happened to the boys as fact or describe how they might have died. If the learner seems upset, pause and offer to stop.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote before you start. In 1483, two young princes disappeared from the Tower of London. What do you think happened to them?
>
> A) Their uncle, the new king, got rid of them
> B) They escaped and lived in secret
> C) Nobody can know for sure

Don't react to the vote.
