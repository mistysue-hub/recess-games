# Case 002, Part 2: Did They Make It?

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: Alcatraz, part 2: nobody has proven whether the three men survived the bay. The learner sorts tested facts from claims nobody can check and decides whether the file should stay open.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- sort evidence into tested facts, untested claims and expert opinions
- explain the difference between "possible" and "proven," using the raft experiments

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Unsolved. Never say whether the men survived or call any verdict wrong; "we can't tell yet" is honest.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote before you start. The three Alcatraz escapers were never found. What do you think happened to them?
>
> A) They drowned in the bay
> B) They made it and lived in secret
> C) Nobody can know for sure

Don't react to the vote.
