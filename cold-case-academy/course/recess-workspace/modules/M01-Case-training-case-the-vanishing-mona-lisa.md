# Training Case: The Vanishing Mona Lisa

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: The training case: in 1911 the Mona Lisa vanished from the Louvre, and the learner plays a detective game to find out who took it. The answer is known, so it's practice for the unsolved cases.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- retell how the Mona Lisa was stolen and how it was finally found
- name at least two wrong theories the police chased

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Training case with a known answer; never reveal it before they have played.

**Hook:**

> Welcome to Cold Case Academy, Detective. Your first case: in 1911, the world's most famous painting vanished from a museum in Paris. Quick vote before you start. Who do you think stole it?
>
> A) A master thief hired by a millionaire
> B) A famous artist who hated old paintings
> C) A handyman who used to work at the museum

Don't react to the vote.

**Game:** https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/cold-case-academy/the-missing-smile/
Backup if it won't load: https://raw.githack.com/mistysue-hub/recess-games/main/cold-case-academy/the-missing-smile/index.html. Stuck? One nudge: “Which place haven't you checked yet?” or “Which two clues might fit together?” Never name a suspect or verdict.
