# Case 001, Part 1: The Man in the Last Row

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: The first unsolved case: in 1971 a man hijacked a plane, collected $200,000 and vanished, and nobody knows who he was. The learner rebuilds that night from witness statements.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- put the main events of the hijacking in the right order
- explain why two witnesses who agree are stronger evidence than one who doesn't

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Unsolved. Never say who D.B. Cooper was or whether he survived; Part 2 covers survival.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote before you start. A man hijacks a plane, collects a pile of money, and then does something no hijacker had ever done in the U.S. What do you think it was?
>
> A) He jumped out of the plane with a parachute
> B) He hid on the plane and walked off with the crew
> C) He gave all the money back

Don't react to the vote.
