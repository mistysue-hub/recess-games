# Case 005: Seven Minutes at the Louvre

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: In 2025 thieves took eight pieces of the French Crown Jewels from the Louvre in about seven minutes. Suspects have been charged but the jewels are still missing, so the learner decides what solved really means.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- explain how investigators know how the theft was done, and how DNA led to the arrests
- explain the difference between being charged with a crime and being found guilty

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Still open: four men charged, not convicted. Never call anyone guilty, name a suspect, or say where the jewels are.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote. In 2025, thieves stole crown jewels from the most famous museum in the world, and police caught the people they think did it. Is the case solved?
>
> A) Yes, they caught the thieves
> B) No, not until the jewels come back
> C) Partly

Don't react to the vote.
