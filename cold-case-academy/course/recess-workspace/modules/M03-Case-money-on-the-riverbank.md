# Case 001, Part 2: Money on the Riverbank

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: D.B. Cooper, part 2: a kid found some of the ransom money on a riverbank in 1980. The learner tests five real suspects against the evidence and decides whether Cooper could have survived.

Estimated time: 35 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- explain how the FBI proved the beach money was the ransom
- give at least one clue that suggests Cooper survived and one that suggests he didn't

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Unsolved. Never say whether Cooper survived or call any suspect Cooper or any verdict wrong; "we can't tell yet" is honest.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote. Nine years after Cooper jumped, a kid digging on a beach found some of his money. How much of the $200,000 do you think was there?
>
> A) Almost all of it
> B) About $5,800
> C) Just one $20 bill

Don't react to the vote.
