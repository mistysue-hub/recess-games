# Final Report: Your Verdict

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → report builder link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: Graduation: the learner builds a final case report on any case, with facts, a theory, evidence for and against, and an honest verdict. "We can't tell yet" is an honest answer.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, report builder about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- tell facts apart from claims, expert opinions and guesses
- support a theory with evidence, and name the evidence against it

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Capstone: they pick a case they've played (not M08 if skipped). No theory is wrong; never call any case solved.

**Hook:**

> Last vote of the academy. What makes a detective's report trustworthy?
>
> A) It names who did it
> B) It sticks to evidence, including the evidence against its own theory
> C) It sounds very confident

Don't react to the vote.

**Report builder:** https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/cold-case-academy/final-report/
Backup if it won't load: https://raw.githack.com/mistysue-hub/recess-games/main/cold-case-academy/final-report/index.html. Stuck? One nudge: “Could someone check that?” or “Does that card make your theory more likely, or less?” Never name a suspect or verdict.
