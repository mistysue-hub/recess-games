# Case 003, Part 1: 81 Minutes

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: In 1990 two thieves took 13 artworks from a Boston museum in 81 minutes. The learner uses the motion-detector record to find the fact that doesn't fit.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- explain how the motion-detector record lets investigators rebuild a night nobody else saw
- name what was taken and what was strangely left behind

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Unsolved. Never say who the thieves were or where the art is, and never suggest the night guard was guilty (never charged).

**Hook:**

> Quick vote. Two thieves spent 81 minutes inside a famous Boston museum. How do you think they got in?
>
> A) They broke in through a window
> B) They dressed as police officers, and a guard let them in
> C) They hid inside until the museum closed

Don't react to the vote.
