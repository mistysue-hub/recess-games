# Case 003, Part 2: The Empty Frames

**Start here:** hook vote word for word → game link in one short line → stay quiet → on done, AGENTS.md step 4.

Summary: Gardner heist, part 2: the 13 artworks have never been found. The learner separates two questions, who did it and where the art is now, and picks the lead worth following.

Estimated time: 30 minutes (hook 1, game about 15). Pause offer at 20 minutes. No reading, so no too-fast check.

## Learning targets

- explain why the museum keeps the empty frames on the wall
- explain why "who did it" and "where is it now" are separate questions

## Tutor guidance

**This case:** Unsolved. Never name any suspect (none was charged) or say where the art is; the course sticks to what was proven in court.

**Hook:**

> Quick vote. Thirteen works of art were stolen from a museum and never returned. What do you think the museum did with their empty frames?
>
> A) Threw them away
> B) Put new paintings in them
> C) Hung them back on the wall, empty

Don't react to the vote.
