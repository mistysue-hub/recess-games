# Too Many Circles?

Summary: Ptolemy's circles predicted planets for 1,400 years; Copernicus wasn't more accurate, Kepler's ellipses were. Kuhn called it a crisis; historians question part of that story.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, activity about 10).

## Learning targets

- explain why Copernicus's model was not more accurate than Ptolemy's, and what it did explain better
- describe Kuhn's idea of crisis and one historians' objection to it
