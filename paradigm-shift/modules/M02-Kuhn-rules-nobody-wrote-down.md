# Rules Nobody Wrote Down

Summary: A paradigm is a science's shared big ideas, model examples and unwritten rules, learned mostly by example. Electricity research in the 1700s shows science before and after Franklin gave it common ground.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, game about 10).

## Learning targets

- describe what a paradigm includes, in their own words
- explain how electricity research changed once researchers shared Franklin's picture

> Quick vote before you read. Can you learn all the rules of a game just by watching people play, with nobody ever explaining them? Yes or no? How sure are you, 0 to 100?
