THE DUEL

Wet-sponge duel! You and a rival start 10 steps apart, each with ONE sponge. Take turns: THROW, or STEP one step closer. A hit wins. A miss loses, because the other player just walks up and drops their sponge on your head. The closer you get, the better your aim.
