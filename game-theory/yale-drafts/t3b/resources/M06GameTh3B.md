# GAME 18: FOG OF WAR
You pick UP, MIDDLE or DOWN. Then Shade picks LEFT or RIGHT.
Points (you, Shade): UP-LEFT 4, 0. UP-RIGHT 0, 4. MIDDLE-LEFT 0, 4. MIDDLE-RIGHT 4, 0. DOWN-LEFT 1, 2. DOWN-RIGHT 0, 0.
Rounds 1-3: Shade sees your move. Rounds 4-9 (fog): Shade sees DOWN, but can't tell UP from MIDDLE.

Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3b-m06-fog-of-war.html
