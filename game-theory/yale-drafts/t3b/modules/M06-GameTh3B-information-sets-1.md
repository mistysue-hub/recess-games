# M06 Information Sets and Sub-Game Perfection, Part 1
Summary: Play a game where your rival sometimes can't see your move, and watch your score change.
Estimated time: 29 minutes (play 10, clip 19)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3b-m06-fog-of-war.html
2. Play 9 rounds: 3 clear, then 6 in the fog.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- See how what a player can SEE changes the game.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT3B-M06. These are Polak's own numbers. Clear: best is DOWN, 1 point. Fog: mixing UP and MIDDLE about half and half averages about 2. Ask once, before the clip: "Why did you score more in the fog?" Clip: https://youtu.be/D7aDIZ-KPEU?end=1136 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 information sets: the points a player can't tell apart, drawn with dotted lines; the same game with and without fog. The next part is the next module.
