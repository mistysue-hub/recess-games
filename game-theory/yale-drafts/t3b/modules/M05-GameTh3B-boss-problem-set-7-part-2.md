# M05 BOSS: Yale Problem Set 7, Part 2
Summary: Finish off the Island Host: a two-firm patent race, plus an optional rock-array challenge.
Estimated time: 25 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset7_1_0.pdf
2. Do Question 3 (parts a and b). Question 4 is an optional bonus.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Solve a race by working backward from the finish.
- Weigh the good and bad sides of patents.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE ISLAND HOST, round 2, HP 2. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 3: two firms each four steps from a patent, with costs per step; the likely outcome and why patents exist. Optional Question 4 (bonus, no HP): the rock-array game from Lecture 15 for any size grid. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
