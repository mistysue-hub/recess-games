## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE LAST STANDER, HP 4. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 1: all sub-game perfect equilibria of a war of attrition when the cost is bigger than the prize, pure and mixed. Question 2: a bank-run game drawn as a tree with information sets, its sub-game perfect equilibria in two cases, and whether a minimum payout stops the run. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
