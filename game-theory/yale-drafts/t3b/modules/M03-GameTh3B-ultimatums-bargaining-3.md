Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "With endless rounds and very fast offers, the split heads toward A) 50/50, B) everything to the first proposer, or C) everything to the second." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/XpEmuxE4qWk?t=2864 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 47:44 the endless-rounds result: the first proposer gets 1/(1 + factor); 54:29 the assumptions behind it and why real bargaining has delays. Rematch (Rocky only): endless rounds. With factor 0.8 the first proposer gets 1/(1 + 0.8), about 56%. With factor 0.9, about 53%. Why does it get closer to 50/50? (Faster offers mean less is lost by waiting.)
