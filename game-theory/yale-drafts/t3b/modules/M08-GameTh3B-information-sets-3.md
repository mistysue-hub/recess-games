## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Can a Nash equilibrium ask a player to do something silly in a part of the game nobody reaches? A) Yes. B) No." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/D7aDIZ-KPEU?t=2999 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 49:59 sub-games: a game inside the game that starts at one point and doesn't cut an information set; Polak's three-player example; 70:17 sub-game perfect equilibrium, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): Polak's three-player game. Player 1 picks A (payoffs 1, 0, 0) or B. After B, Players 2 and 3 play: Up-Left (0, 1, 1), Up-Right (0, 0, 2), Down-Left (0, 0, -1), Down-Right (2, 1, 0) (Player 2 picks Up or Down, Player 3 Left or Right). The sub-game's equilibrium is Down-Right; Up-Left isn't (Player 3 would switch to Right for 2). So Player 1 picks B and gets 2. The boss rounds (Problem Set 8) are the next modules. The learner explains it in their own words.
