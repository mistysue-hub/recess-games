## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak's matchmaker sends a couple to a class, but they can't talk first and like different classes. If they each guess with the mixed equilibrium, do they meet A) always, B) about 4 times in 9, or C) never?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/hw014me49ks?t=1642&end=2977 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 27:22 the matchmaker and the two classes; 34:31 the three sub-game perfect equilibria. Rematch (Rocky only): the couple mix as Polak finds (David picks his favorite 2/3 of the time, Nina picks hers 2/3 of the time), so they meet 4/9 of the time. A meeting gives the matchmaker +1. If a miss costs only -0.5 instead of -1, should he send them? (Yes: 4/9 - 0.5 x 5/9 = 1/6, more than 0.) The learner explains it in their own words.
