## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE ISLAND HOST, HP 6. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 1: six contestants split six gold coins by proposals and votes; what should the first proposer offer? Question 2: a railroad deciding whether to build when the workers' union can hold it up (sunk costs, take-it-or-leave-it wages, arbitration, fixes). No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
