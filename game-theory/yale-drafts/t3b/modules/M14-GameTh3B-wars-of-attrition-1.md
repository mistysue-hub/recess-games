# M14 Wars of Attrition, Part 1
Summary: Fight three holdouts for a prize where every round of fighting costs you.
Estimated time: 34 minutes (play 10, clip 24)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3b-m14-holdout.html
2. Play 3 matches. Each round, FIGHT or QUIT.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Notice how costs already spent pull people into longer fights.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT3B-M14. Stan fights each round with chance 100/(100 + 75), about 57%; at that mix fighting and quitting are worth the same, about 0 on average. Ask once, before the clip: "Did coins you'd already spent change what you did next?" Clip: https://youtu.be/wR8dqbc6mlQ?end=1444 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 the class fight-or-quit game ($1 prize, 75 cents a round); 17:39 real wars of attrition: trench warfare, a satellite TV fight, bribery contests. The next part is the next module.
