## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak says the thing that decides how a game should be drawn is A) timing, who moves first in time, B) information, who knows what, or C) the payoffs." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/D7aDIZ-KPEU?t=1136&end=2999 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 18:56 trees to tables and back; a strategy is a plan for every information set; 35:11 finding Nash equilibria, two of which fail backward induction. Rematch (Rocky only): Prisoner's Dilemma as a tree: Player 1 picks Up or Down, then Player 2 picks Left or Right without seeing it. Payoffs: Up-Left (2, 2), Up-Right (-1, 3), Down-Left (3, -1), Down-Right (0, 0). What happens? (Right is always better for Player 2, Down for Player 1: outcome (0, 0).) Would it change if Player 2 could see Player 1's move? (No: Right is still better either way.) The learner explains it in their own words.
