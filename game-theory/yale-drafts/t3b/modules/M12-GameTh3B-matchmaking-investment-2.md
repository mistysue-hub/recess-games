A) All three. B) One. C) None." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/hw014me49ks?end=1642 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 a three-step game with three Nash equilibria; only one survives, because the others rely on a threat that isn't credible. This rewinds to the start of the lecture. Rematch (Rocky only): Player 1 picks A (payoffs 1, 0) or B. After B, Player 2 picks C (0, 2) or D. After D, Player 1 picks E (3, 1) or F (-1, -1). Work backward: Player 1 picks E; Player 2 compares C (2) with D then E (1) and picks C; Player 1 compares A (1) with B then C (0) and picks A. Sub-game perfect: (A and E, C).
