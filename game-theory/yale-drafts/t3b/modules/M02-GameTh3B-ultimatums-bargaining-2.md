## Learning targets

## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "In a two-round bargain, if the pie shrinks a lot after the first offer is turned down, who has the edge? A) The first proposer. B) The second proposer. C) Nobody." Answer: A (the first proposer keeps 1 minus the shrink factor). Clip: https://youtu.be/XpEmuxE4qWk?t=855&end=2864 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 14:15 adding rounds where the pie shrinks; two, three, four and ten rounds. Rematch (Rocky only): the pie shrinks to half each round (factor 0.5). Two rounds: first proposer keeps 1 - 0.5 = 50%. Three rounds: keeps 1 - 0.5 x (1 - 0.5) = 75%. The learner works backward from the last round. The learner explains it in their own words.
