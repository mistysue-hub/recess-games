Summary: Finish off the Negotiator: traffic jams, toll booths and the best road split.
Estimated time: 25 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset8_3_0.pdf
2. Do Question 3 (parts a to e), on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Compare the traffic people choose with the traffic that's best for everyone.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE NEGOTIATOR, round 2, HP 5. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 3: commuters choosing roads, the Nash equilibrium with and without a toll, the planner's best split, the toll that gets there, and the time at the toll booth. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
