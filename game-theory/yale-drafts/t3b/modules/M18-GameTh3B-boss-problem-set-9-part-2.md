# M18 BOSS: Yale Problem Set 9, Part 2
Summary: Finish off the Last Stander: a dating game in the suburbs, plus an optional money-burning bonus.
Estimated time: 25 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset9_1_0.pdf
2. Do Question 3 (parts a and b). Question 4 is an optional bonus.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Draw a game as a tree and as a table, then solve it.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE LAST STANDER, round 2, HP 2. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 3: a movie-choice dating game, its tree and table, and when every sub-game perfect equilibrium sends Cheryl to the movies. Optional Question 4 (bonus, no HP): burning money and deleting weakly dominated strategies. This is the last module of Game Theory 3. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
