## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "In nature, can three types keep cycling forever, like rock-paper-scissors? A) Yes, and Polak gives a real animal example. B) No, one always wins. C) Only in computer games." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/5sK9W0viyu0?t=3000 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 50:00 what V/C means; 55:39 can these models be tested; then rock-paper-scissors cycles in real animals, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): in a rock-paper-scissors world, suppose almost everyone plays Rock. Which type grows next (Paper), then which (Scissors), then which (Rock again)? Why can no single type be stable? (Each one is beaten by another.) The boss round (Problem Set 5) is the next module. The learner explains it in their own words.
