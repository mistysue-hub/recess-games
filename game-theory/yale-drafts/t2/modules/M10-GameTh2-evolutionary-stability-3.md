## Learning targets

## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Is every Nash equilibrium evolutionarily stable? A) Always. B) Not always. C) Never." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/er9KvYn4ldk?t=2552&end=3780 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 42:32 Nash equilibria that are not evolutionarily stable. Rematch (Rocky only): Plain/Plain 2, Plain vs Fancy 2, Fancy vs Plain 2, Fancy/Fancy 1. In all-PLAIN, Fancy mutants tie against Plain (2 = 2), so check the tie-breaker: natives must do strictly better against the mutant than the mutant does against itself. Plain vs Fancy 2 beats Fancy vs Fancy 1, so the mutants die out and all-PLAIN is stable. (If those two were equal, it would not be stable.) Contrast with World 3 in the game, where the tie-breaker went the other way. The learner explains it in their own words.
