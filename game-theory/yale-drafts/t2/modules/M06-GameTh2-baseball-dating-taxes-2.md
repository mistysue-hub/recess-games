Summary: Learn two ways to read a mixed strategy, then find the audit rate for a new fine.
Estimated time: 30 minutes (clip 24, rematch 6)
Sittings: 1

## Learning targets
- Explain a mixed strategy as a belief about the other player.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "A mixed strategy doesn't have to mean flipping a coin. It can also mean A) what the other player believes you'll do, B) cheating, or C) changing the rules." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/9b2AMWwMToc?t=1421&end=2824 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 23:41 players really flipping coins; 28:17 mixing as beliefs about what the other side will do. Rematch (Rocky only): tax 10, fine 40. Cheating stops paying when audit chance x (10 + 40) reaches 10, so at 1 in 5 (20%). The learner sets up that equation. Then: what audit rate if the fine is 90? (1 in 10, 10%.) The learner explains it in their own words. The boss round (Problem Set 4) is the next module.
