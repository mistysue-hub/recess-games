## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak asks which side of the road a population drives on. Can 'everyone drives on the left' be stable? A) Yes. B) No, only the right works. C) Only with police." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/5sK9W0viyu0?end=1850 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 tests for a stable one-type population and a stable mix; driving on the left or right; other examples from nature. (This rewinds to the start of the lecture.) Rematch (Rocky only): prize 4, fight cost 10. A hawk earns x(4 - 10)/2 + (1 - x)4; a dove earns (1 - x)2, where x is the hawk share. Setting them equal gives x = 0.4, so 40% hawks (prize divided by cost). The learner sets up the equation. The learner explains it in their own words.
