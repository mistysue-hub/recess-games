# M08 Evolutionary Stability: Cooperation, Mutation, Equilibrium, Part 1
Summary: Seed three beetle worlds with mutants and predict whether they take over.
Estimated time: 36 minutes (play 10, clip 26)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t2-m08-mutant-invasion.html
2. Run 3 worlds. Predict before each run.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Say what it means for a strategy to resist invasion by a few mutants.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT2-M08. World 1: all-SHARE gets invaded. World 2: whichever species the learner picks, mutants die out. World 3: all-PLAIN gets invaded (mutants tie against natives but win against each other). Ask once, before the clip: "Which species could a few mutants not break into?" Clip: https://youtu.be/er9KvYn4ldk?end=1540 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 a worked example of an evolutionarily stable strategy. The next part is the next module.
