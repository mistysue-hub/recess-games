C) Only in big populations." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/er9KvYn4ldk?t=1540&end=2552 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 25:40 discussion; 30:42 evolutionarily stable strategies are always Nash equilibria. Rematch (Rocky only): Share or Grab world (Share/Share 3, Share vs Grab 0, Grab vs Share 5, Grab/Grab 1). Is all-SHARE stable? No: a Grab mutant earns 5 against Share, more than Share's 3. Is all-GRAB stable? Yes: a Share mutant earns 0 against Grab, less than Grab's 1. The learner explains it in their own words.
