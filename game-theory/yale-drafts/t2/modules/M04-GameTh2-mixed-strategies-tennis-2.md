## Learning targets

## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Using the same numbers as your game, Polak finds Venus's best mix. Is it A) 50% left, B) 70% left, or C) 100% left?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/unvJofv0wl8?t=1625&end=3580 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 27:05 no pure-strategy equilibrium; about 46:00 the mixed equilibrium (Venus 70%, Serena 60%); about 54:00 checking that nobody gains by switching. The clip stops before Serena's improved backhand. Rematch (Rocky only): the RIGHT-vs-RIGHT number rises from 20 to 30. Best plan: LEFT about 67% (two-thirds), winning about 63% either way. Worked: 50p + 90(1 - p) = 80p + 30(1 - p), so p = 2/3. The learner sets up the equation. The learner explains it in their own words.
