## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT2-M05; best rates: 40% with fine 20 (budget 920), 20% with fine 50 (budget about 1,030). Ask once, before the clip: "What happened to your best audit rate when the fine went up?" Clip: https://youtu.be/9b2AMWwMToc?t=2824 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 47:04 mixing as a split in a large population; 59:52 policy, including tax audits. Polak's conclusion: a bigger fine lowers the audit rate, while the share who cheat stays the same. The game is a simpler commit-first version, so its cheater counts differ; say so if asked. The next part is the next module.
