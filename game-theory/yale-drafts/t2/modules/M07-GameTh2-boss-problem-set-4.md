# M07 BOSS: Yale Problem Set 4
Summary: Beat the Spymaster: Yale's mixed-strategy homework on changing payoffs, a spy's route and honest advice.
Estimated time: 35 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset4_2_0.pdf
2. Work the 3 questions on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Use mixed strategies on Yale's own homework.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE SPYMASTER, HP 3. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each question answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Problem Set 4 has 3 questions: how a mixed equilibrium shifts when payoffs change (the tax-audit idea), a spy choosing a route with an avalanche risk, and a repair expert whose advice may not be honest. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
