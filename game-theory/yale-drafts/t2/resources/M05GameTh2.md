# GAME 10: AUDIT OR NOT
100 taxpayers owe 10 coins each. You pick an audit rate each year. Each audit costs 3.
Honest: pays 10. Cheater not audited: pays 0. Cheater audited: pays 10 plus the fine.
Taxpayers know your audit rate, give or take a little, and cheat when it seems worth the risk.

Years 1-2: fine 20. Years 3-4: fine 50.
