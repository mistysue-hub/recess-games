# GAME 8: SORTING TOWN
100 houses: 45 blue families, 45 pink, 10 empty, mixed at random.
A family moves to a random empty house if too few neighbors (up to 8 around it) match its color.
Picky levels: 25%, 33%, 50%, 75%. The same-color score starts near 50%.

Pick a picky level, predict the final score, then run the town. 3 towns.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t2-m01-sorting-town.html
When the game ends, tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
