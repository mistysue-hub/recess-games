# GAME 9: SERVE AND RETURN
You serve LEFT or RIGHT. Ace leans before the serve and knows your plan.

Chance you win the point:
              Ace LEFT   Ace RIGHT
Serve LEFT      50%        80%
Serve RIGHT     90%        20%

Ace leans whichever way holds you lower. Try 3 plans, 20 serves each.
