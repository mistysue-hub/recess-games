# GAME 12: HAWK-DOVE ARENA
Birds meet in pairs over a prize worth 4 points.
HAWK vs HAWK: both fight, each gets half the prize minus half the fight cost.
HAWK vs DOVE: hawk takes all 4, dove gets 0.
DOVE vs DOVE: they share, 2 each.
More points, more chicks. Arenas 1-2: fight cost 6 (arena 2 starts at the opposite end). Arena 3: the fight cost is a mystery to work out.

Pick a starting share of hawks, predict where it ends, then watch 40 generations.
