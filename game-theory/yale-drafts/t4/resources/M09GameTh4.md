# GAME: HIRING DESK
You hire for Boring Bank. Gus is worth 50 to the bank; Bo is worth 30. You can't tell them apart.
One year of MBA school costs Gus 5 and Bo 10.01. School teaches nothing here.
Your rule: at least N years of MBA gets paid 50; less gets 30. Each worker gets the MBA only if it leaves them better off.
3 rounds: set N, predict whether Bo fakes it, then open the doors.
