# GAME: JAR OF COINS
4 jars of coins. Everyone gets a guess: the true amount plus or minus up to 12 coins.
Sealed bids: the highest bid wins, pays its bid, and keeps the coins. Profit = coins in the jar minus the bid.
You see only your own guess. 5 rivals bid too.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t4-m12-jar-of-coins.html
When the game ends, tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
