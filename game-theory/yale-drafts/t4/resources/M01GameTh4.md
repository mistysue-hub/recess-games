# GAME: LAST ROUND
Two matches of Cooperate or Cheat. Each round you and your rival choose at the same moment.

| You \ Rival | COOPERATE | CHEAT |
|---|---|---|
| COOPERATE | You 2, Rival 2 | You -1, Rival 3 |
| CHEAT | You 3, Rival -1 | You 0, Rival 0 |

Match 1 vs Mira: exactly 5 rounds, and everyone knows round 5 is the last.
Match 2 vs Dex: at least 3 rounds, then a coin decides after each round (3 chances in 4 to keep going).
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t4-m01-last-round.html
When the game ends, tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
