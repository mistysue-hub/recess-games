# M15 BOSS: Yale Practice Problems on Asymmetric Information
Summary: Outwit the Informant: Yale's practice set on signals and a second-price auction.
Estimated time: 35 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/practice_problems_on_asymmetric_information_0.pdf
2. Do all 4 problems (Problem 3 is marked hard), on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Use signaling and auctions on Yale's own practice set.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE INFORMANT, HP 4 (one per problem). Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Problem 1: a firm's price may signal its costs to a would-be rival. Problem 2: a candidate's choices may signal her chances. Problem 3 (hard): costly class signals and a pass/fail test; a good try counts. Problem 4: a second-price auction between two bidders. Yale posts solutions, but Rocky does not use them. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
