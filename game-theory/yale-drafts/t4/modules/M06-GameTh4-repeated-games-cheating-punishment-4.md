## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "A firm abroad worries its workers will steal materials. If the job could last a long time, must the firm pay A) more than for a one-time job, B) less, or C) the same?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/NBKr-wK-TEQ?t=3189 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 53:09 repeated moral hazard: outsourcing to Freedonia, where the going wage is 1 and stealing pays 2; 73:53 conclusions, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): the honest worker earns W every round; a thief gets 2 once, then the going wage 1 forever. Honesty pays when W is at least 2 - d (d = chance the job continues). Check Polak's points: d = 0 gives 2; d = 1/2 gives 1.5. Twist: d = 0.8 gives 1.2, only a 20% premium. The boss round (Problem Set 10) is the next two modules. The learner explains it in their own words.
