# M12 Asymmetric Information: Auctions and the Winner's Curse, Part 1
Summary: Bid on four jars of coins against rivals who trust their guesses a little too much.
Estimated time: 32 minutes (play 10, clip about 22)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t4-m12-jar-of-coins.html
2. Bid on 4 jars. You see only your own guess.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Spot the winner's curse: the winner is often the one who guessed too high.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT4-M12; check each jar's winner and profit (jar value minus winning bid). Ask once, before the clip: "Who usually won the jars, and did they make money?" Clip: https://youtu.be/pJAhnc8VuDo?end=1300 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 common versus private values; 8:16 Polak auctions a jar of coins to the class, explains the winner's curse, and links it to oil leases and sports free agents. The clip ends near his line about how to correct for it; the cut is estimated, so confirm by watching. The next part is the next module.
