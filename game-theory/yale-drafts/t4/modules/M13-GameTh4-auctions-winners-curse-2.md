## Learning targets

## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "To avoid the winner's curse, should you bid A) above your guess, B) exactly your guess, or C) below your guess?" Answer: C. Clip: https://youtu.be/pJAhnc8VuDo?t=1300&end=2558 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: fixing the curse: bid as if you already know your guess was the highest; a second, smaller jar auction where most bidders shaded, but not enough. Rematch (Rocky only): ten bidders, each guess off by up to 12 either way. The highest of ten such errors averages about 12 x 9/11, close to +10. So if your guess is 60, bidding as if you won means treating the jar as worth about 50, and bidding below that. With 6 bidders it is about 12 x 5/7, near +9. The learner explains it in their own words.
