# M18 BOSS: Yale Final Exam, Part 3 (optional)
Summary: Optional round 3 against the Final Boss: Exclusive, a club that votes new members in one at a time.
Estimated time: 30 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. This round is optional. To skip it, tell Rocky "skip" and go to step 4.
2. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/final_exam_2007_0.pdf and do Question 4 (a, b), on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Use backward induction on a game of votes.
- Explain each step, not just the outcome.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Optional boss round, no time limit: if the learner says skip, Rocky accepts it with no pressure and the module is done. Boss: THE FINAL BOSS, round 3, HP 2. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 4: existing members vote on nominees in order; a asks why a nominee gets in when there are three members; b (most of the points) asks how the whole game plays out using everyone's rankings. Work backward from the last vote (Template 3 ideas). Most credit is for the explanation. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
