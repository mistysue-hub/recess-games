# M01 Repeated Games: Cooperation vs. the End Game, Part 1
Summary: Play two matches of Cooperate or Cheat, one with a known last round and one with a coin-toss ending.
Estimated time: 33 minutes (play 10, clip 23)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t4-m01-last-round.html
2. Play both matches. Match 1 has 5 known rounds; Match 2 ends on a coin toss.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- See why cooperation falls apart when everyone knows the last round.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT4-M01. Payoffs are Polak's: both cooperate 2 each; both cheat 0; cheater 3, cooperator -1. Mira copies the learner's last move but always cheats in round 5; Dex cooperates until the learner cheats once, then cheats forever. Ask once, before the clip: "Why did Mira cheat in round 5 no matter what?" Clip: https://youtu.be/zNxVVVk5It0?end=1364 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 Polak plays this game with pairs of students; 16:40 cooperation unravels from the last round back, and the lame-duck effect. The next part is the next module.
