## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "With these payoffs, how big must the chance of another round be for Grim Trigger to keep cooperation going? A) At least 1 in 3. B) At least 9 in 10. C) Any chance at all." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/NBKr-wK-TEQ?end=1761 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 the Grim Trigger check: cheating gains 1 today but loses 2 every round after, which works when the chance of continuing is 1/3 or more. Rematch (Rocky only): cheating against a cooperator now pays 4 instead of 3. Gain today: 4 - 2 = 2. Loss: the chance d times 2/(1 - d). Need 2 is at most 2d/(1 - d), so d is at least 1/2. A bigger temptation needs more patience. The learner explains it in their own words.
