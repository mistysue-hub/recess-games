## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Optional boss round, no time limit: if the learner says skip, Rocky accepts it with no pressure and the module is done. Boss: THE FINAL BOSS, round 1, HP 6. Rocky opens with a short boss taunt; each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP, shown after each. Yale's final covers the second half (Templates 3A, 3B and 4); this part leans on 3A and 3B. Question 1: claims about burning your boats, mixed versus pure deviations, the sponge duel, and tuition and signaling. If stuck, optional clips: 1a https://youtu.be/aIq0E3OmNz4?t=2965&end=3353 (Lecture 14, why fewer options can help); 1c https://youtu.be/SE7kP7XZuV4?t=2044&end=2742 (Lecture 16, the duel). No key: Rocky checks reasoning, asks one follow-up, corrects only what he is sure of.
