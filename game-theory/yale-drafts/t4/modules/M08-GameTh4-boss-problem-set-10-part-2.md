# M08 BOSS: Yale Problem Set 10, Part 2
Summary: Finish off the Cartel: keeping a price cartel together in a game that never ends.
Estimated time: 35 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset10_0_0.pdf
2. Do Questions 3 and 4 (4c is optional), on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Find how patient firms must be to keep a cartel together.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE CARTEL, round 2, HP 4 (3a, 3b, 4a, 4b; 4c is a bonus). Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 3: a price war between two firms, first played once, then forever with a continuation chance d: find the lowest d that keeps the monopoly price. Question 4: the lowest d for Grim Trigger in another repeated game, then the worst possible punishments; 4c (optional) asks for the shortest punishments that still work. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
