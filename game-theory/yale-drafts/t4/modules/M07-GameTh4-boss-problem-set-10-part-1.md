Summary: Take on the Cartel: using different equilibria as rewards and punishments, and a game played twice.
Estimated time: 35 minutes
Sittings: 1

1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset10_0_0.pdf

## Learning targets

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE CARTEL, HP 7 (one per part). Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Question 1: two partners invest, then split the money; a to d look for subgame perfect equilibria that use one equilibrium as a reward and another as a punishment, then the case where investments can't be seen (Template 3 ideas). Question 2: a game played twice; find its equilibria, build one that plays a good outcome in round 1, and argue why it resists renegotiation better than the class example. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
