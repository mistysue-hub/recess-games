# M03 Repeated Games: Cheating, Punishment, and Outsourcing, Part 1
Summary: Back a robot in three strategy tournaments and find out what makes cooperation win.
Estimated time: 29 minutes (play 10, clip about 19)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t4-m03-strategy-tournament.html
2. Run 3 tournaments. Before each, back the robot you think will win.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- See how the chance of another round decides whether cheating or cooperating wins.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT4-M03. Winners: 20% chance to continue, SHARK; 60% and 90%, COPYCAT and GRUDGE tie. Ask once, before the clip: "What changed between the tournament SHARK won and the ones it lost?" Clip: https://youtu.be/zNxVVVk5It0?t=3195 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses) (Lecture 21, 53:15 to the end). By time: 53:15 the game now ends on a coin toss; the Grim Trigger strategy, which is the game's GRUDGE robot. The next part is the next module.
