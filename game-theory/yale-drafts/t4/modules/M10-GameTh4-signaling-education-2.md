## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak calls this model of education A) optimistic, B) pessimistic, or C) neutral?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/RWxTJsTbK2w?t=3576 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 59:36 conclusions, to the end: a signal must cost types differently; qualifications inflate when they get cheaper; nobody learns anything in this model, and the weaker workers end up worse off. Rematch (Rocky only): if school cost Bo only 6 per year: Gus still goes for up to 4 years (50 - 20 = 30); Bo is stopped only when 50 - 6n is below 30, so n at least 4. A 4-year rule just works. If it cost both 5 per year, no rule can separate them (like Polak's 'dancing on the table'). The learner explains it in their own words.
