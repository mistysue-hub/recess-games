Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "In a second-price auction, where the winner pays the second-highest bid, what should you bid for something you value at 40? A) 40. B) Less than 40. C) More than 40." Answer: A. Clip: https://youtu.be/pJAhnc8VuDo?t=2558 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 42:38 first-price sealed, second-price sealed, open rising and open falling auctions; 58:35 which auction raises the most money, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): you value a bike at 40 in a second-price auction. Why is bidding 35 never better than 40? If the top rival bid is under 35 you win either way and pay the same; if it is between 35 and 40, bidding 35 loses a bike you would have bought below your value; if it is over 40 you lose either way. This is Final Exam Question 5a. The boss rounds are next. The learner explains it in their own words.
