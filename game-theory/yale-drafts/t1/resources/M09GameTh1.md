# GAME 5: BANK RUN
Six depositors, 10 coins each. Each day all choose at the same moment.

WITHDRAW: 10 coins, always.
KEEP: 15 coins if at least 4 of the 6 KEEP. Otherwise the bank fails and you get 0.

Day 1: calm. Day 2: a rumor. Day 3: depositors copy what they saw on day 2.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m09-bank-run.html
