# GAME 1: SHARE OR GRAB
5 rounds, a new partner every round. Each partner promises to SHARE, but nothing makes them keep it. You both pick SHARE or GRAB at the same moment.

| You \ Partner | SHARE | GRAB |
|---|---|---|
| SHARE | You 3, Partner 3 | You 0, Partner 5 |
| GRAB | You 5, Partner 0 | You 1, Partner 1 |

Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m01-share-or-grab.html
