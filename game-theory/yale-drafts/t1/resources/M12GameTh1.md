# GAME 6: FACTORY OUTPUT
Two toy factories. Each round both choose crates (1 to 9) at the same moment.
Price per crate = 14 minus total crates. Cost = 2 per crate.
Profit = (price minus 2) x your crates.

Rusty opens with 6, then plans around what you made last round. 4 rounds.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m12-factory-output.html
When the game ends, tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
