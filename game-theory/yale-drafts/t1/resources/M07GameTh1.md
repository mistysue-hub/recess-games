# GAME 4: PENALTY KICK
10 kicks. Kick LEFT or RIGHT while the goalie dives at the same moment.

Chance you score:
              Goalie LEFT   Goalie RIGHT
Kick LEFT        55%            90%
Kick RIGHT       85%            45%

Kicks 1-5: Wall-E has a habit (the game shows a scouting report). Kicks 6-10: Scout dives toward your most-used side.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m07-penalty-kick.html
When the game ends, tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
