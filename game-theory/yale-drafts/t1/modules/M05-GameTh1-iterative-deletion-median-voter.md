# M05 Iterative Deletion and the Median Voter
Summary: Place a candidate on a line and see why rivals crowd the middle.
Estimated time: 40 minutes (play 8, clip 27, rematch 5)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m05-pick-your-position.html
2. Play 3 rounds against rivals who stand at 3, 8, then 5.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip, then the rematch.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Find the position that wins the most votes against a rival.
- Use deletion round after round to predict where candidates land.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, rematch, done. Pasted line starts GT-M05; best spots: 4 vs 3 (70%), 7 vs 8 (70%), 5 or 6 vs 5 (50%). Ask once: "Where did the best spot go when the rival moved?" Clip: https://youtu.be/kqDu0RVWTYw?end=1645 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: deleting 1 and 10, then 2 and 9, then 3 and 8, leaving 5 and 6. Rematch (Rocky only): Rocky at 2, best reply 3 (80%). Rocky at 9, best reply 8 (80%). The learner explains why. The boss round (Problem Set 1) is the next module.
