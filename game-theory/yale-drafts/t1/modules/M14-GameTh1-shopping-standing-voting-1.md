# M14 Shopping, Standing and Voting on a Line, Part 1
Summary: Fight a price war with identical sodas, then with loyal fans.
Estimated time: 38 minutes (play 10, clip 28)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m14-price-war.html
2. Play 6 rounds against Penny's Pops. The rule changes after round 3.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Explain why identical sellers drive prices down to cost.
