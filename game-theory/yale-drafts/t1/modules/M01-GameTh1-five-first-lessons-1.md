# M01 Five First Lessons, Part 1
Summary: Play five rounds of Share or Grab, then see why one move beats the other no matter what.
Estimated time: 30 minutes (play 10, clip 20)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m01-share-or-grab.html
2. Play all 5 rounds. Each partner is new and picks at random.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Spot a move that beats another no matter what your partner does.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT-M01; check totals against resources/M01GameTh1.md. Every partner promises to SHARE; 3 keep it and 2 break it (GRAB), in random order. Ask once, before the clip: "Did your moves change? What do you notice in Replay the Tape?" Clip: https://youtu.be/nM3rTU927io?t=580&end=1773 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 9:40 parts of a game; 21:38 dominant versus dominated. Part 2 is the next module.

## Resources and surfaces
Game link above. resources/M01GameTh1.md. Backup: https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159/lecture-1

## Completion criteria
- Pasted a valid 5-round result in chat before opening the clip.
- Explained in their own words why Grab beats Share whatever the partner does.
