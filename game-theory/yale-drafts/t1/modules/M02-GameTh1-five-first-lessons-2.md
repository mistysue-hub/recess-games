# M02 Five First Lessons, Part 2
Summary: Finish the Share or Grab clip, then try a rematch where no move is best every time.
Estimated time: 20 minutes (clip 12, rematch 8)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the clip and watch to its end.
2. Rocky gives you a rematch with a twist. Answer in chat.
3. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- See why smart choices can still give a bad result (Prisoner's Dilemma).
- Change the payoffs and watch the game change.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak's class can talk before choosing. Will a promise to Share hold? A) Yes, talking fixes it. B) Only if the promise can be enforced. C) Only between friends." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/nM3rTU927io?t=1773&end=2500 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 29:33 why a deal fails without enforcement; 33:35 the Prisoner's Dilemma, and contracts need teeth. Rematch (Rocky only): Share/Share 3 each; Grab/Grab 1 each; Grab vs Share: grabber 2, sharer 0. No move is dominant: Share is best if the partner Shares, Grab if the partner Grabs. The learner explains it in their own words.
