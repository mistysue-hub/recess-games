## Tutor guidance
Flow: clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Do real penalty kickers A) always aim at their stronger side, B) mix their sides, or C) always aim down the middle?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/YYUPc-cfPyc?t=914&end=1799 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 15:14 problems with the model; 24:06 the formal definition of best response. Rematch (Rocky only): If the goalie always dives LEFT, best kick is RIGHT (85%). If you always kick RIGHT, best dive is RIGHT (you score 45%). Each side's best reply flips, so no fixed pair is steady. Best mix: about 53% LEFT for the kicker, about 60% LEFT for the goalie, 69% scored. There is no problem set for this lecture. The clip covers best responses to beliefs; the idea of mixing comes from this rematch, so Rocky teaches it here. The learner explains it in their own words.
