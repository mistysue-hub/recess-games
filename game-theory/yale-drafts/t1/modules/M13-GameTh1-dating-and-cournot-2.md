# M13 Nash Equilibrium: Dating and Cournot, Part 2
Summary: Find where the best-response lines cross, then re-solve with a higher cost.
Estimated time: 22 minutes (clip 14, rematch 8)
Sittings: 1

## Learning targets
- Find the Nash equilibrium where both best responses meet.
- Explain, using the game's 3-and-3 deal, why both firms would earn more making less, but won't.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Where will Polak's two best-response lines cross? A) Where both make a lot. B) Where both make the same amount. C) Where one firm makes nothing." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/7oASpaBdDMs?t=2390&end=3208 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: drawing both best-response lines and where they cross. Rematch (Rocky only): each crate now costs 5. Best reply is (9 minus the rival's crates) / 2. Equilibrium: 3 crates each, price 8, profit 9 each. There is no problem set for this lecture. The learner explains it in their own words.
