# M03 Other People's Shoes, Part 1
Summary: Guess two-thirds of the average, then see why some guesses are always a mistake.
Estimated time: 21 minutes (play 8, clip 13)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m03-two-thirds.html
2. Lock in a number from 1 to 100 for two rounds. Five classmates pick too.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Delete guesses that are dominated: another guess always does at least as well.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT-M03; check the average and target (two-thirds of the average of six). Ask once, before the clip: "Why did your round 2 number change, or not?" Clip: https://youtu.be/qQ3kFydI_xQ?t=2129&end=2880 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 35:29 why high guesses never win; then what rational players assume. Part 2 is the next module.

## Resources and surfaces
Game link above. resources/M03GameTh1.md. Backup: https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159/lecture-2

- Pasted a valid 2-round result in chat before opening the clip.
- Explained why any guess above 67 is dominated: 67 always lands at least as close to the target.
