# M09 Nash Equilibrium: Bad Fashion and Bank Runs, Part 1
Summary: Run a bank for three days, then see how real bank runs happen.
Estimated time: 30 minutes (play 8, clip about 22)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m09-bank-run.html
2. Play 3 days as one of 6 depositors.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Explain how fear alone can make a bank fail.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT-M09; best reply: KEEP if 3+ of the other 5 keep, else WITHDRAW. Ask once, before the clip: "What decided if the bank survived?" Clip: https://youtu.be/Ac2vCCuf8-s?t=3191 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 53:11 to the end: bank runs and other examples where people get stuck in the worse outcome. Part 2 is the next module.
