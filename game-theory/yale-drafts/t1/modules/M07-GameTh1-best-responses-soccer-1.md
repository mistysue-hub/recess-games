# M07 Best Responses in Soccer and Partnerships, Part 1
Summary: Take ten penalty kicks against goalies who guess, then see the penalty kick as a game.
Estimated time: 23 minutes (play 8, clip 15)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m07-penalty-kick.html
2. Take 10 kicks. The goalie in kicks 6-10 reads your habits.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Find your best response to what the other player does.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT-M07; check kicks against dives. Wall-E dives LEFT 80%, so the best reply is RIGHT (about 77% vs 62%). Ask once, before the clip: "What changed in your kicks once the goalie started reading you?" Clip: https://youtu.be/YYUPc-cfPyc?end=914 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 penalty kicks as a game; what the kicker should do against each dive. Part 2 is the next module.
