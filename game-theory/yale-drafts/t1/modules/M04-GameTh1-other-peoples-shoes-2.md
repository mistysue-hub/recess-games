# M04 Other People's Shoes, Part 2
Summary: Peel back layers of "I know that you know" and solve a three-quarters rematch.
Estimated time: 25 minutes (clip 18, rematch 7)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the clip and watch to its end.
2. Rocky gives you a rematch with a twist. Answer in chat.
3. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Delete guesses round after round.
- Say what each round of thinking assumes about other players.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "If everyone in the room is rational, and everyone knows that everyone knows it, all the way down, what should they guess? A) 33. B) 22. C) 1." Answer: C. Clip: https://youtu.be/qQ3kFydI_xQ?t=2880&end=3937 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: deleting dominated guesses again once everyone knows nobody picks above 67; layers of "I know that you know". The clip stops before the hat demonstration of mutual versus common knowledge. Rematch (Rocky only): the target becomes three-quarters of the average. Guesses above 75 are dominated by 75. Once nobody picks above 75, guesses above 56 (three-quarters of 75 is 56.25) are dominated too. The learner names both cutoffs and what each round assumes. The learner explains it in their own words.

## Completion criteria
- Named the first cutoff in the rematch (75) and why guesses above it are dominated.
- Named the second cutoff (about 56) and what it assumes about others.
