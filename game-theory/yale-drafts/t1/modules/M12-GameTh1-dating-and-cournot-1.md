# M12 Nash Equilibrium: Dating and Cournot, Part 1
Summary: Run a toy factory against a rival and find where output settles.
Estimated time: 29 minutes (play 8, clip 21)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/m12-factory-output.html
2. Play 4 rounds against Rusty's Robots.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Then open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Find a best response when your choice and a rival's share one price.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT-M12; price = 14 minus total crates, profit = (price minus 2) x crates. Ask once, before the clip: "Where did Rusty's numbers end up, and why?" Clip: https://youtu.be/7oASpaBdDMs?t=1117&end=2390 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 18:37 Cournot setup; working out each firm's best response. Part 2 is the next module.
