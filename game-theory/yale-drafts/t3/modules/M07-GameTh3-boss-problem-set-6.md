# M07 BOSS: Yale Problem Set 6
Summary: Beat the Central Banker: Yale's homework on news timing, rules versus discretion, and a landlord's deal.
Estimated time: 40 minutes
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open it: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/problemset6_1_0.pdf
2. Work the 3 questions (9 lettered parts) on paper or in a doc.
3. Paste your answers with your reasoning in the chat, one part at a time.
4. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Use backward induction and commitment on Yale's own homework.
- Show your reasoning, not just the answer.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: work, paste, follow-up, done. Required boss round, no time limit. Boss: THE CENTRAL BANKER, HP 9. Rocky opens with one short taunt from the boss, and each part answered with reasoning knocks off 1 HP; Rocky shows the HP after each one. Problem Set 6 has 3 questions: TV stations choosing a 6 or 7 o'clock news slot at the same time and then in turn; a government and unions setting inflation and wages, with and without commitment; a landlord choosing a worker's share of output. No key is used. Rocky checks each answer has reasoning, asks one follow-up, and corrects only what he is sure of.
