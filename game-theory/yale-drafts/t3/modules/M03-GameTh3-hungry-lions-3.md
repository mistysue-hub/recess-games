## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak describes an invading army that burned its own boats. Why? A) To keep warm. B) To remove the option to retreat, which changes how the enemy acts. C) It was an accident." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/5tGh7wd2zOU?t=2669&end=3666 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 44:29 commitment: collateral, burning boats, and a doomsday machine nobody knew about. Rematch (Rocky only): invaders land. If they keep their boats, the defenders choose FIGHT or GIVE UP; if the defenders fight, invaders RETREAT (invaders 0, defenders 2) rather than fight (invaders -1). If the invaders BURN the boats, they must fight: defenders fighting gets defenders -1 and invaders 1; giving up gets defenders 0 and invaders 3. Boats kept: defenders fight, invaders get 0. Boats burned: defenders give up, invaders get 3. Burning is better. The learner works backward from the last move. The learner explains it in their own words.
