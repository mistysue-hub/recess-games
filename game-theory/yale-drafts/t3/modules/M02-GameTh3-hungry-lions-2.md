## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "When a borrower might run off with a big loan, what does Polak say lenders do? A) Lend more. B) Lend less, which hurts both sides. C) Stop lending forever." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/5tGh7wd2zOU?t=1077&end=2669 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 17:57 moral hazard and small loans; 29:50 incentive design: shares, contracts and piece rates. Rematch (Rocky only): a boss sets pay, then a worker picks effort. Lazy: 6 items, no effort cost. Hard: 15 items, effort cost 30. Each item is worth 8 to the boss. Flat pay 50: lazy gives the worker 50, hard gives 20, so lazy; boss earns 48 - 50 = -2. Pay 5 per item: lazy gives 30, hard gives 75 - 30 = 45, so hard; boss earns 120 - 75 = 45. The boss should pay per item. The learner works from the worker's choice back to the boss's. The learner explains it in their own words.
