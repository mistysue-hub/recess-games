## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Polak's class plays a stone-taking game. Starting with two equal piles, who can always win? A) The first player. B) The second player. C) Nobody, it's luck." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/aIq0E3OmNz4?t=2965 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 49:25 when moving first is not an advantage (rock-paper-scissors, cutting a cake); 55:53 the two-pile stone game, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): two piles, take any number from one pile, last stone wins. Piles 9 and 6: who wins, and what's the first move? (First player: take 3 from the 9 pile to make them equal, then copy.) Piles 5 and 5? (Second player.) The boss round (Problem Set 6) is the next module. The learner explains it in their own words.
