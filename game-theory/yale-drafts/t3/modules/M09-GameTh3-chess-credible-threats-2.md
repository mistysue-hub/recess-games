## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Does Zermelo's theorem say A) the first player always wins chess, B) one side can force a win or a draw but it doesn't say which, or C) chess is pure luck?" Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/Bm8lM73lTmk?t=1026&end=2427 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 17:06 why the proof works for games of any length; 31:20 the rock game where taking the last rock loses. Rematch (Rocky only): a single row of 5 rocks, rock 1 in the corner. Taking a rock removes it and every rock to its east. Whoever takes the last rock (the corner) loses. Who wins? (The first player: take rock 2, which removes rocks 2 to 5, leaving only the corner for the other player.) The learner explains it in their own words.
