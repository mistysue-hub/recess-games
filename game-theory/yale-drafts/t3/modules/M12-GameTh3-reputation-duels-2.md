## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "In the sponge duel, who should throw first? A) The better shot, right away. B) Nobody, until a certain distance. C) The worse shot, right away." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/SE7kP7XZuV4?t=2742 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 45:42 the two facts and the rule: throw when your chance now plus the rival's chance one step closer is at least 1; 71:24 why players in class throw too early. Rematch (Rocky only): both players hit with chance 1 - d/10 at distance d. Using the rule, at what distance should the first throw come? (Chance now + rival's chance one step closer = (1 - d/10) + (1 - (d - 1)/10) is at least 1 when d is 5 or less, so at 5 steps.) After the rematch Rocky may share Yale's write-up: https://oyc.yale.edu/sites/default/files/discussion_of_duel_0.pdf The learner explains it in their own words.
