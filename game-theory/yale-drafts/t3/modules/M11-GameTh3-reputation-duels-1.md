# M11 Reputation and Duels, Part 1
Summary: Fight three wet-sponge duels and learn exactly when to throw.
Estimated time: 30 minutes (play 10, clip 20)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m11-the-duel.html
2. Fight 3 duels. Each turn, THROW or STEP closer.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Find the right moment to act when acting early or late can both lose.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT3-M11. With perfect play the first throw comes at 6 steps (vs Dana), 5 (vs Walt) and 5 (vs Steven). By time: 25:18 the sponge duel in class; 34:04 the real question is WHEN to act (duels, bike-race breakaways, product launches). This starts mid-lecture; Part 3 rewinds to the start.
