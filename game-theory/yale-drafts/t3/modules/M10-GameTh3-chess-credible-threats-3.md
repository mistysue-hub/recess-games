## Tutor guidance
Flow: bet, clip, rematch, done. Before the clip, Rocky offers a bet (learner picks A, B or C and locks it in; settle it after the clip): "Can a threat you'd never really carry out keep a rival away? A) Yes, always. B) Only if it's believable; backward induction ignores empty threats. C) Threats never matter." Answer: B. Clip: https://youtu.be/Bm8lM73lTmk?t=2427 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 40:27 games of perfect information and what a full strategy is (a plan for every point, even ones never reached); 61:56 an entrant and an incumbent: the threat to fight is not credible, to the end. Rematch (Rocky only): Polak's entry game: Out gives (entrant 0, incumbent 3); In then Fight gives (-1, 0); In then Don't fight gives (1, 1). Now change fighting so it pays the incumbent 2 instead of 0. Is the threat believable now? (Yes: 2 beats 1, so the incumbent fights and the entrant stays out.) The learner explains it in their own words.
