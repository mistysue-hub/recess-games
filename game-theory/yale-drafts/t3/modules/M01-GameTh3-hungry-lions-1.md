# M01 Moral Hazard, Incentives and Hungry Lions, Part 1
Summary: Decide whether the top lion should eat, then learn to solve a game from the end backward.
Estimated time: 36 minutes (play 8, clips 28)
Sittings: 1

## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m01-hungry-lions.html
2. Play 5 prides. For each, choose EAT or DON'T EAT first.
3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.
4. Open the clip.
5. When Rocky says you're done, tap Submit.

## Learning targets
- Solve a take-turns game by backward induction: start at the last move.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT3-M01. The biggest lion should eat when the pride has an odd number of lions (2 no, 3 yes, 4 no, 7 yes, 10 no). Ask once, before the clips: "Which lion did you have to think about first?" Clips: https://youtu.be/5tGh7wd2zOU?t=3666 (61:06 to the end: Polak's hungry lions) then https://youtu.be/5tGh7wd2zOU?end=1077 (0:00 backward induction with a cash-in-a-hat game) (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). The first clip jumps ahead; say so. The next part is the next module.
