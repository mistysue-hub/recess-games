## Start here
1. Open the game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m08-last-stone.html

3. Tap "Copy my results for Rocky" and paste them in the chat.

## Learning targets
- See that in games like this, one player can always force a win.

## Tutor guidance
Flow: play, paste, clip, done. Pasted line starts GT3-M08. Winning choice: unequal piles, go first and make them equal; equal piles, go second (5-3 first, 4-4 second, 7-2 first). Ask once, before the clip: "When should you choose to go first?" Clip: https://youtu.be/Bm8lM73lTmk?end=1026 (Ben Polak, Open Yale Courses). By time: 0:00 Zermelo's theorem: in games like this one side can force a win or a tie; 10:17 the idea of the proof. The next part is the next module.
