# GAME 14: FIRST MOVER
Rematch with Rusty's Robots. Price per crate = 14 minus total crates. Cost = 2 per crate.
Profit = (price minus 2) x your crates.

Round 1-2: you build first and Rusty sees your number. Round 3: Rusty builds first.
Round 4: you both choose at the same time.
Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m04-first-mover.html
