# GAME 16: THE DUEL
Wet-sponge duel. You and a rival start 10 steps apart with ONE sponge each.
Take turns: THROW, or STEP one step closer. A hit wins. A miss loses.
The closer you are, the better your aim. 3 duels: a better shot, a worse shot, an equal shot.

Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m11-the-duel.html
