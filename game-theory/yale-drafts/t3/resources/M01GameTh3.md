# GAME 13: HUNGRY LIONS
A pride of lions finds a sheep. Only the biggest lion may eat it.
A lion that eats falls asleep, and then the next biggest lion may eat HIM.
Every lion would rather stay hungry than be eaten.
You are the biggest lion. For each pride (2, 3, 4, 7 and 10 lions), choose EAT or DON'T EAT.

Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m01-hungry-lions.html
