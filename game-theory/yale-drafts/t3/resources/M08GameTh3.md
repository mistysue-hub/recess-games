# GAME 15: LAST STONE
Two piles of stones. On your turn, take any number of stones (at least 1) from ONE pile.
Whoever takes the very last stone wins.
Before each match, look at the piles and choose who goes first. 3 matches against Pebble.

Game: https://mistysue-hub.github.io/recess-games/game-theory/t3-m08-last-stone.html
