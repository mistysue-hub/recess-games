Mix Master

How it works. Steve Martin played beautiful banjo with a fake arrow through his head. Something high and something low, side by side, and he treated the whole thing with total seriousness. That collision is called juxtaposition.

Each round gives you something serious and dignified. Tap the partner that makes the funniest mix. After each tap, the card shows the course's pick and why. You can disagree. Most rounds have more than one good answer, and any strong mix counts as a match; only the dull pairing misses. Taste counts. The last screen counts how often you and the course matched.

Hard mode: before you tap, say your own funnier partner out loud. Then see if the game has it.
