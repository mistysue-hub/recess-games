Who's the Joke On?

How it works. Steve Martin's stage guy was a confident fool in a white suit. The laugh always landed on the character he was playing, never on a real person who couldn't answer back.

Ten short jokes, one at a time. For each one, tap who the joke is on: the character (the made-up person telling it or in it) or a real person (an actual person or group who isn't there to answer back).

After each pick, the card shows the course's call. You can disagree; the last screen counts how often you and the course saw it the same way.
