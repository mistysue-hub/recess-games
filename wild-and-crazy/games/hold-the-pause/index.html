Hold the Pause

How it works. Each bit is told one beat at a time, and the balloon is the room's tension: it grows with every beat you hold. Steve Martin never let the air out with a punchline. He walked off while the tension was at its peak.

Every bit has one perfect moment to walk off: the beat where the tension is highest. After it, the next beat would only repeat the joke or explain it, and the air leaks out. Tap Hold to hear the next beat, or Walk off to end the bit right there.

Six bits, up to five beats each. Walk off on the perfect beat for 2 points, one beat early or late for 1.
