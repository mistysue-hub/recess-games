The Long Road

How it works. Six stops from Steve Martin's early career are shuffled below the road; tap them in the order they happened, earliest first, to build the road one slot at a time.

A wrong tap tells you what he learned at that stop and lets you try again, and after two misses on the same slot the right card lights up.
