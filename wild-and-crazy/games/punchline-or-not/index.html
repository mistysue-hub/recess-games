Punchline or Not?

How it works. Eight tiny bits. Each has a setup and two endings. One ending is a regular punchline. The other is Steve Martin's way: no punchline, just something flat, true or beside the point, said straight and then left hanging.

Tap the ending that is Steve's way. After each pick, the card shows the course's call. You can disagree; the last screen counts how often you and the course saw it the same way.

That's the set!

Type done to Rocky and read him the line in the box.

All bits written for this course. Steve Martin is not involved.
