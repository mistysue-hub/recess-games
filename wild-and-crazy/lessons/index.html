Wild and Crazy · Open Mic Night

Learn Comedy by Studying
Steve Martin

8 lessons · read, watch, play, write

This course teaches comedy by studying Steve Martin's career and what he's said in public about his work. He isn't involved, and nothing here is copied from his books or albums.
Module 1

What If There Were No Punchlines?

Steve's biggest idea: build tension and never release it. Write one joke two ways.
Module 2

The Ten Years Nobody Came

How Steve got good before anyone noticed. Write an honest opener about something you're bad at.
Module 3

The Confident Fool

The man in the white suit: totally confident, totally wrong. Write a character who never realizes.
Module 4

Hold It... Hold It...

Tension as a tool: the endless banjo tuning, silence, counting beats. Write a bit with no release.
Module 5

Arrow Through the Head, Banjo in Hand

High meets low: juxtaposition. Write three pairings and build one into a bit.
Module 6

Every Word Counts

Steve the writer: precision and cutting to the bone. Cut a joke to half its length.
Module 7

The Notebook

Set structure, the inner critic, and taking notes on your own work. Write a three-bit mini set.
Module 8

Your Set

Assemble and perform your own set. The stage is yours.

Each lesson: a question, a reading, a short clip, a game, and writing prompts. About 25 minutes.
