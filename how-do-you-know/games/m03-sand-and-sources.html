How Do You Know? · Lock 3

Two rounds

Round 1, the Sand Square. Take the boy's place in Plato's Meno. You start with a square of four square feet and have to find a square exactly twice as big. Predict, test, then draw the answer yourself in the sand.

Round 2, the Source Sorter. Every claim gets two questions:

1. Could you know it without checking the world? Yes means a priori, no means a posteriori.
2. Is it true just because of what the words mean? Yes means analytic, no means synthetic.

Then the claim drops onto a board with four corners. One corner is the one Kant fought over. One is the corner nearly everyone thinks stays empty.

You lock in every answer before the game shows you anything.
