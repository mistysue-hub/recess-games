How the tower works

Every belief sits on a reason. Every reason sits on another reason. Tap Ask why and watch the tower grow downward, one block at a time, until you reach the end of the chain.

Agrippa's trilemma says a chain can end only three ways:

Foreverthere's always another reason underneath
Circlea reason loops back to something above it
Stopthe chain rests on a belief with no reason under it

For each tower, say how it ended and which theory says that ending can be fine. Then, at the Theory Desk, match what people say to the theory they hold.
