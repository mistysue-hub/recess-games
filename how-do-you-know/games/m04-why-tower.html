How the tower works

The question: If every belief needs a reason, where does the chain of reasons end, and is that ending good enough?

Every belief sits on a reason, and that reason sits on another. Each time you're asked why?, pick one of three reasons. A chain can end only three ways:

Foreverthere's always another reason underneath
Circlea reason loops back to something above it
Stopit rests on a basic belief with nothing under it. (“Because I said so” is this ending. Parents have used it for centuries.)

Build the tower, then say how it ended. A guide stays on screen.

You'll miss some. Everyone does, even philosophers. A miss gets you a hint and a second try.

Why?

Your tower is built. How does this chain end?

How to decide. Pick a reason. Does it need another reason? → keep going. Does it lean on the belief you started with? → circle. Did you see or feel it yourself, with nothing under it? → stop.
