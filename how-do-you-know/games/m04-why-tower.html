How the tower works

Every belief sits on a reason. Every reason sits on another reason. You build the tower: each time someone asks why?, pick one of three reasons and it drops in underneath. Some reasons call for another reason, and some end the chain, sometimes sneakily.

Agrippa's trilemma says a chain can end only three ways:

Foreverthere's always another reason underneath
Circlea reason loops back to something above it
Stopthe chain rests on a belief with no further belief under it

When your tower is done, say how it ended and which objection hits it hardest. Try to end each of the three towers a different way. Then, at the Theory Desk, match what people say to the theory they hold.
