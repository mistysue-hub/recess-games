Two worked examples

Dev checks his library's app, which reads the shelf scanners, and believes the book he wants is on shelf 4. It is. Believe yes, True yes, Justified yes, and the app is right because the scanner saw the book there. Reasons and truth are connected: KNOWLEDGE.

Smith from the reading believes "the person who gets the job has ten coins in his pocket." All three gauges read yes, but his reasons were about Jones and the truth came from his own pocket. They only meet by luck: NOT KNOWLEDGE. That's a Gettier case.
