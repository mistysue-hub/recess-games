Your confidence vs. what happened

Sorry, chicken. Saddest example in the course. It's also why the chicken is famous.

Bertrand Russell told this story in 1912, in The Problems of Philosophy. His point: expecting tomorrow to match yesterday can mislead. The chicken needed, in Russell's words, “more refined views as to the uniformity of nature.”
