How the ladder works

Every belief has to climb past Descartes's three doubts. The higher the rung, the stronger the doubt.

1 · Senses They sometimes fool you about tiny, distant or blurry things.
2 · Dream You could be dreaming, so anything a dream could fake falls.
3 · Deceiver A powerful trickster could fool you about everything, even sums.
Untouchable No doubt, however strong, knocks it out.

Your job: find the first rung that knocks each belief out. Just ask these four questions in order and stop at the first yes:

  1. Is it far away, tiny or blurry? Yes → Senses
  2. Could a dream fake it? Yes → Dream
  3. Is it math or logic (true even in a dream)? Yes → Deceiver
  4. Is it that you exist or are thinking? Yes → Untouchable

Example. "That ship on the horizon is tiny." Far away? Yes. Stop: it falls at Senses.
