Over the past months, you've told Rocky a lot about yourself. Today, Rocky pulls it all together into one place: your Future Passport. It's like the highlight reel of your trailer, and it's the ticket that gets you into the feature film.

2

Your Passport stamps

Every module you finished earned a stamp. Rocky has been keeping them safe in your profile (next to his calendar, which he has also been keeping very safe).

Today he'll show you the whole Passport, stamp by stamp. Skipped a module? No problem. It stays open anytime, and you can grab that stamp later.

You may also have some bonus stamps. Those are for real-world try-outs, like a volunteer shift, a curious conversation with an adult who has an interesting job, or an optional activity you finished. Each bonus stamp comes with a short note about what you did. Those notes become great material for the main course.

3

What's in your Passport

Here's what Rocky has been saving:

  • 🎨 Stuff You Love: the things you lose track of time doing, plus your interest letters if you took the quiz.
  • 💪 Stuff You Rock At: your strengths, including the ones you didn't know you had.
  • ☀️ Your Daydream Day: your perfect Tuesday at 25.
  • 🦒 Job Safari picks: your "I didn't know that was a job!" favorites.
  • 🛣️ Lanes you're curious about: 🎓 college or university, 🔧 trade school or apprenticeship, 💼 career, 🚀 working for yourself, 🌍 crossing borders. Any number of them is fine, including all of them.
  • 🧗 Your Experience Log: everything you've tried so far.
  • 🗺️ Your People Map: your supporting cast.
  • 🌱 Head-Start Habits: your running list and any early-timeline paths you're curious about.
  • 🎓 Dual credit: what you found out about college classes in high school, what's available where you live, and what your parents said.

Rocky will read it back to you. Then you get to fix anything that's changed. Changing your mind is not just allowed. It's expected. That's what growing looks like.

4

2–3 small goals for this year

Now, pick two or three small goals for this year. Small means really small. Things like:

  • "Try one new club or volunteer spot (with a parent setting it up)."
  • "Have one curious conversation with someone on my People Map."
  • "Find out if our community college has dual credit for homeschoolers."
  • "Keep my running list going every month."
  • "Save a little from every birthday card."

These are not promises carved in stone. They're more like sticky notes. Rocky will check in on them now and then. (He has already found the perfect spot on his calendar. Several spots, actually.)

Coming soon: the feature film

Sneak Peek was the trailer. The feature film is the main course, What Comes Next? It goes deeper on every lane and helps you build a real plan with your family.

When to start: at about 2 years from graduation. Rocky keeps an eye on your timeline and will cheerfully let you know when it's showtime. When you start, he'll use your Future Passport to give you a head start, so you won't have to repeat everything you've already told him.
