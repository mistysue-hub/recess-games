Welcome to the previews

You know the part at the movies before the actual movie, when they show trailers for things coming soon? Dramatic music, a deep voice saying "In a world..."?

This is that. But the movie is your future, and honestly, it looks pretty great.

You're about two or three years away from graduating. That might feel like forever, or it might feel like next Tuesday. Either way, you're right on time to start noticing things: what you love, what you're good at, and what kinds of jobs and adventures are out there. (Spoiler: there are way more than you think. Somebody's job is naming paint colors. Somebody else tests roller coasters.)

2

The best news: zero pressure

Let's get this out of the way: you do not have to choose a career right now. Not today, not this year. You don't even need a favorite yet.

Sneak Peek is all about curiosity. We'll explore, try a few things, laugh a little, and build some easy habits that make the next couple of years smoother. "I don't know yet" is not just allowed here. It's the whole point.

3

Meet Rocky

Rocky is your guide for this adventure. He's friendly, curious, honest, and very, very enthusiastic about calendars. He has promised to keep the calendar talk to a minimum in Sneak Peek.

(He will not succeed. Please be patient with him.)

As you go, you'll tell Rocky your answers in chat instead of writing them down. He'll remember them, notice patterns, and help you spot things about yourself you might have missed. At the end, he'll collect it all into your Future Passport, with a stamp for every module you finish.

And Rocky's not the only one in your corner. Besides your family, you've got your Recess crew: friends, guides and mentors who are all rooting for you. Fun fact: most of them didn't have it figured out at your age either. Ask them sometime. The stories are great.

A first peek at the lanes

Most paths after high school fit into one of three lanes. You don't pick one now. You just get to wave hello.

🎓 College / University. Four-year colleges, community colleges, even universities in other countries. Great for people who love digging deep into a subject.

🔧 Trade School / Apprenticeship. Learning a skilled, hands-on job, often while getting paid to learn. (Yes, someone pays you to learn. Still wild.) Electricians, chefs, aircraft mechanics, medical assistants and many more.

💼 Career. Jumping into work, training on the job, the military, or a planned gap year.

Plus two bonus features:

  • 🚀 Working for yourself, like starting a business or building games and websites.
  • 🌍 Crossing borders, for anyone dreaming of studying or working in another country, or coming to the US from somewhere else.

No lane is better than another. Lots of people mix them or switch later. The best one is the one that fits you.

6

🏡 Homeschool Edge

In homeschool, your family decides when you graduate (following your state's homeschool rules). That's a superpower: your timeline can fit you. Rocky will ask for your planned graduation date, or just the year if that's all you know. If you're already under two years out, he'll point you to the feature film, and you can still finish Sneak Peek if you're enjoying it.
