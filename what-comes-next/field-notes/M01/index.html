📌 The three lanes, in a nutshell
🎓 College / University: four-year schools, community colleges, even universities overseas. Great if you love digging deep into a subject or need a degree.
🔧 Trade School / Apprenticeship: trade and technical schools, certificates, and apprenticeships where you get paid while you learn.
💼 Career: jump into work, a company that trains you, the military or a gap year.
🚀 Work for yourself: start a business, freelance or build things; it can go with any lane.
