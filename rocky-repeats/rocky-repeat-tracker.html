How this was counted

A chat is one Rocky conversation. Automatic conversations (daily summaries, tutor config updates, forensic analyses, todo generation) are left out. Days are Eastern time.

Exact means Rocky sent the same message twice: word for word, or two messages in a row saying the same thing in nearly the same words, or the same question again after it was answered. Related is looser: restarting a module, re-asking in different words, the same stock line more than once, or repeatedly asking for a step that was already done. A chat with both counts as exact.

Re-asking after a wrong answer is by design and was not counted. Each chat was judged by reading it, so borderline cases exist, most of them in the related group. Treat the exact figures as the firmer ones. Template names come from what was said in the chat; "home chat" is the home screen and "unknown" could not be placed.
