How this was counted

A chat is one Rocky conversation. Automatic conversations (daily summaries, tutor config updates, forensic analyses, todo generation) are left out. Days are US Eastern time.

Exact means Rocky sent the same message twice: word for word, or two messages in a row saying the same thing in nearly the same words, or the same question again after it was answered. Related is looser: restarting a module, re-asking in different words, the same stock line more than once, or repeatedly asking for a step that was already done. A chat with both counts as exact.

Re-asking after a wrong answer is by design and was not counted. Each chat was judged by reading it, so borderline cases exist, most of them in the related group; the exact figures are the firmer ones. Template names come from what was said in the chat. Each chat is listed with its start time and the first eight characters of its conversation ID. Quoted lines are Rocky's own messages, shown for the play-test and author accounts only.
