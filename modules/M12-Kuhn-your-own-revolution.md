# Your Own Revolution

Summary: The learner tests Kuhn's pattern on a belief they changed, maps every thinker in the course on two questions, and plans the course's next edition. Last module.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, activity about 10).

## Learning targets

- describe a personal change of mind using (or questioning) Kuhn's pattern
- compare the course's thinkers on what makes science trustworthy and what it's for

## Tutor guidance

**Hook (word for word):**

> Quick vote before you read. Think of something you once believed firmly and don't now (a rule, a fact, a game strategy). What changed your mind: new evidence, a person, or just time?
