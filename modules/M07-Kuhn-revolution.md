# Revolution: The Drifting Continents

Summary: Wegener's 1912 continental drift was rejected for lacking a mechanism; sea-floor evidence in the 1950s–60s led to plate tectonics.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, activity about 10).

## Learning targets

- explain why geologists rejected continental drift at first
- describe the evidence that led to plate tectonics

## Tutor guidance

**Hook (word for word):**

> Quick bet before you read. Alfred Wegener said the continents drift. How long until most geologists agreed: about 5, 20 or 50 years?
