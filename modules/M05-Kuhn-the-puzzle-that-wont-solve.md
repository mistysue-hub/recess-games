# The Puzzle That Won't Solve

Summary: Uranus drifted off course and Neptune rescued Newton in 1846; the same rescue for Mercury, the planet Vulcan, failed until Einstein's 1915 theory. Anomalies usually get fixes first.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, game about 10).

## Learning targets

- explain what an anomaly is, using Uranus and Mercury
- describe why scientists try to rescue a theory before replacing it

## Tutor guidance

**Hook (word for word):**

> Quick bet before you read. In 1846, astronomers blamed Uranus's odd orbit on an unseen planet, and Neptune turned up. Later they tried the same trick on Mercury. Did it work again? Yes or no?
