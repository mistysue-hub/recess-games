# Seeing What You Expect

Summary: In a 1949 experiment, trick playing cards took four times longer to recognize and were first seen as normal; Kuhn says paradigms shape what scientists can see.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → play the game first, then open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, game about 10).

## Learning targets

- explain the 1949 trick-card experiment and its results
- connect expectation and observation to Kuhn's idea of a paradigm

## Tutor guidance

> Quick vote before you read. True or false: if a playing card flashes in front of you, you'll notice right away if its color is wrong. Then say how sure you are, 0 to 100.
