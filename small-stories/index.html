Small Stories, Big Moves

A school year of reading and writing short stories, one craft move a week, ending with three finished stories, two of them rewritten, and a portfolio. Built on MIT's free Writing and Reading Short Stories course.

Each weekTwo sittings, about 2–3 hours. Read a story and fill in the six-question notebook; then learn one move and make it on your Story Desk.
MIT expectsThree stories, exercise journal, written critiques, two substantial revisions, a final portfolio. Every week below shows which MIT assignment it serves.
You produceShort pieces that build to Story 1 (week 13), Story 2 (week 23), Story 3 (week 30), and a portfolio you download and keep.
