Reading Notebook

Six questions for any story. Writers ask these of everything they read, for the rest of their lives. One or two sentences each is plenty. Answer from the story, not from a summary of it.

Your notes stay on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it to Rocky. Part of Small Stories, Big Moves, a course inspired by MIT OpenCourseWare 21W.755 (not affiliated).
