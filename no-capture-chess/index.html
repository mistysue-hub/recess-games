Don't Capture! Chess

Regular chess, but the first player to take a piece loses.

How to play
  • All the normal chess moves and rules apply (castling, en passant, promotion, check).
  • The first player to capture any piece loses immediately (en passant counts too).
  • You can still win the normal way: checkmate your opponent.
  • You also win if your opponent has only capturing moves left, because they'd be forced to capture.
  • Stalemate, a 50-move stretch with no pawn move, or the same position three times is a draw.
  • Squares marked with a red ring and ☠ are captures. Clicking one asks you to click again to confirm.
