Liberty or Death · Day I

THE PLANTATION MACHINE

This model is a claim, not a fact. It shows how the colony's money depended on enslaved labor, and how enslaved people pushed back. It leaves out a great deal. Each arrow means "more of this brings more of that" (+) or "more of this brings less of that" (−). Organizing stands for what enslaved people did together: running away, forming maroon bands in the mountains, and building secret networks between plantations. It pushes against the machine. Tap the top half of a circle (▲) to push it up. The model stays at the top of the screen while you answer.

▲ top half of a circle = more · ▼ bottom half = less
