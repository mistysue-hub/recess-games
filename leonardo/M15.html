Page 13: The Storm That Ends Everything

  • Leonardo wrote vivid instructions for painting a world-ending flood.
  • In the middle of it, wolves, foxes and snakes come to "a truce."
  • This is imagination, built with detail.

Page 14: Seashells on a Mountain

  • Everyone said the Flood carried the shells up the mountains.
  • Leonardo checked: a flood would scatter shells, and a cockle moves only 3 to 4 braccia a day.
  • He found shells in living pairs in their own layer, and decided the land was once sea floor.
