Math That Lies

SHARP does math perfectly. It also drops things on the floor.

SHARP does math with the symbols you expect, plus one you may not know:

Program.csC#
1+   add
2-   subtract
3*   multiply
4/   divide
5%   remainder (what's left over after dividing)

Here is the trap. When both numbers are whole numbers (int), SHARP does whole-number division and throws away the leftover part. 7 / 2 is 3. Not 3.5. Not rounded. Chopped. SHARP isn't wrong; it's doing exactly what int means. The leftover goes to %: 7 % 2 is 1.

If at least one number has a decimal point, you get the decimal answer: 7.0 / 2 is 3.5.

Text has tools too. .Length counts the characters. .ToUpper() SHOUTS.

Program.csC#
1string pet = "ferret";
2Console.WriteLine(pet.Length);      // 6
3Console.WriteLine(pet.ToUpper());   // FERRET
