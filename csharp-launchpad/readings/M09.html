Dice Battle

Everything you've learned, in one program you actually want to run twice.

Final mission. You're going to build a game SHARP plays by itself: two fighters, a 20-sided die each, a loop until one drops. You need one new thing: random numbers.

Program.csC#
1Random rng = new Random();
2int roll = rng.Next(1, 21);   // a random number from 1 to 20
3Console.WriteLine(roll);
⚠ HEADS UP
rng.Next(1, 21) gives 1 up to 20. The second number is one past the biggest you want. Yes, really. SHARP's zero-counting habit strikes again.

Here's the shape of the whole game. Read it slowly; every piece is something you've already done.

Type or copy both parts into the editor, part 1 on top and part 2 underneath, before you press Run. Part 1 alone gives a pile of errors, because SHARP doesn't know what a Fighter is yet.

🚨 RED ALERT: BUG HUNT
Run the game. It will run, but something's off: after the loop, nothing says who won. That's not a bug, that's your job. The real bug: run it three or four times. Sometimes a fighter ends with negative health, like -4. Health below zero makes no sense. Why does it happen? Fix it inside TakeHit so Health never drops below 0.
🚀 LAUNCH: FINISH THE GAME
