# Talking Past Each Other

Summary: Ceres and Pluto changed category without changing at all; the word 'planet' did. Kuhn says after revolutions people use the same words differently and talk past each other.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, activity about 10; Extended debate, 10 more).

## Learning targets

- explain why Pluto became a dwarf planet in 2006
- describe how a word's changing meaning can make people talk past each other
