# Kuhn vs. Popper, 1965

Summary: At a 1965 London meeting, Popper said scientists should try to break their theories; Kuhn said commit long enough to test them. Kuhn later named five shared values for choosing theories.

**Start here:** use the level chosen in M01 → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading 6, talk 9, activity 10; Extended debate +10).

## Learning targets

- compare Kuhn's and Popper's views of normal science
- use Kuhn's five values to explain why scientists can rationally disagree
