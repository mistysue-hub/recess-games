# The Textbook Lie

Summary: Three chemists made oxygen and disagreed about what it was; Kuhn says textbooks tidy revolutions out of the story. The learner starts an Anomaly Log.

**Start here:** choose the level (AGENTS.md rule 1) → say the hook word for word → open the reading at that level → AGENTS.md session flow.

Estimated time: 25 minutes (reading about 6, talk about 9, game about 10).

## Learning targets

- explain why "Priestley discovered oxygen in 1774" leaves out most of the story
- describe Kuhn's claim that textbooks hide scientific revolutions

## Tutor guidance

**Hook (word for word):**

> Quick vote before you read. True or false: oxygen was discovered by one person, on one day. How sure are you, 0 to 100?
